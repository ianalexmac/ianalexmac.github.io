The Alaska Energy Trends Report (AETR) summarized electricity data gathered from federal, state, and utility sources. It provided an overview of electricity capacity, generation, consumption, and price trends from 2011 to 2021. A comprehensive report highlighting these trends has not been produced for the state of Alaska since 2013’s Alaska Energy Statistics Report. It is available in two formats: as a PDF and as a digital “web book”.

More details about the Alaska Energy Trends Report can be [found here](https://www.uaf.edu/acep/projects/alaska-electricity-trends-report-2024.php) at the reports overview page, [via this blog post](https://www.uaf.edu/acep-blog/acep-publishes-the-2024-alaska-electricity-trends-report.php) or by visiting the site itself by clicking the image below.
